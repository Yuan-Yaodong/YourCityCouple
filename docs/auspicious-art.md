# Algorithmic Philosophy: "Auspicious Emergence"

## Philosophy

Fortune manifests through the accumulation of small, meaningful moments—each particle a wish, each flow a journey toward destiny. This algorithm expresses the Chinese New Year concept of "福" (fortune) emerging organically from chaos into ordered beauty.

The system begins with scattered particles representing potential—each carrying the weight of possibility. Through layered noise fields mimicking the randomness of life, particles find their paths, clustering where resonance occurs. Like gold coins accumulating or lanterns lighting the night, fortune gathers where intention meets opportunity.

Color emerges from position and velocity—deep reds of prosperity settled, golden wealth in motion, warm ambers of hope. The algorithm runs until equilibrium, revealing a mandala of fortune—each viewer's unique crystallization of auspicious energy.

## Concept

The subtle reference is Chinese New Year fortune symbols: gold coins (元宝), lanterns (灯笼), and the character 福 (fortune) inverted on doorways—traditional imagery understood by Chinese people intuitively, appreciated by all as universal symbols of prosperity and good wishes.
